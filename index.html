02 — About

An independent practice built on standard.

EMOC Architects is an architecture-led design practice based in Abuja, Nigeria, providing services across architecture, interior design, and construction. The practice is led by Founder and CEO Okonkwo Emmanuel Chiemezue.

Every commission is approached with the same standard of care, from initial concept through to completion. EMOC Architects is actively developing new work and welcomes inquiries from clients and collaborators.
